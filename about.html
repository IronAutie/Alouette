About Me

I was born in Haddon Heights, New Jersey to an obstetrician and a school teacher. I was born autistic and didn't talk till six. Growing up I watched both my parents succumb to mental illness, and so was raised instead by books.

My steps to independence began by amateur radio. I taught myself enough algebra at 11 to earn both a license and a two-year grade skip, so I began college at 16.

From that point I had learned sufficient masking to function in the world, to earn a living, to love, to marry, and rear two children.

I was forced to retire in my 40s as a result of not being able to recover from a layoff. I instead began teaching myself how to write stories.

The trilogy you are about to read is my wish for happier times, divided into four main characters reflecting the experiences of my life. It's pure escapism and I hope you will appreciate it taking you from your troubles for a little while.
